# Inventory-and-Supply-Chain-Analytics-
About this project
Analysed 180,000 rows using Excel, Power BI, and Power Query to uncover key insights in inventory and supply chain operations.
Key Insights
•	 Cameras contribute approximately 70% of total sales, making them the primary revenue-driving category.
•	The year 2015 recorded the highest profit across the entire dataset.
•	Debit card is the most preferred payment method.
•	Analysed profit across product categories and payment methods to identify key drivers of profitability.
•	Analysed sales and profit by city, identifying top cities contributing to revenue and regions with low profitability.
•	Identified the top 10 revenue-generating products contributing significantly to the supply chain.

Tools Used
Microsoft Excel: Data Cleaning
Power Query: Data Transformation
Power BI: Data Visualisation

Recommendations
•	Focus on Top 10 Revenue Products:  Ensure consistent availability of top-selling products.
•	Improve Low GMROI Products: Provide higher discounts on slow-moving items.
•	Improve Liquidity: provide selective cash discounts to encourage immediate payments and improve cash flow.
•	Improve Performance in Low-Profit Cities: Optimise logistics and delivery costs in those regions.
•	Optimise Inventory Management: Increase inventory for fast-selling items to avoid stockouts.
•	Learn from 2015 Peak Performance: Analyse what drove high profit in 2015 and try to implement these strategies in operations.

These recommendations aim to improve profitability, optimise inventory utilisation, and reduce operational inefficiencies through data-driven decision-making.
